Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version. To give you Artemis Chat we keep your email address, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password, and the conversations you save. Right now Chat's replies are written by a bootstrap model hosted on Microsoft Azure AI, so the messages you send in Chat go to Microsoft Azure. We don't sell your information, we don't run advertising or analytics scripts on this website, and we don't automatically use your conversations to train Artemis.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have, including under the New York SHIELD Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the EU and UK GDPR.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details. Your email address (stored in lower case) and the date you created the account. Your password is never stored as you typed it: we keep only a salted scrypt hash of it.
  • Conversations. The conversations you have in Artemis Chat: titles, your messages, Artemis's replies, edits and alternative branches, and whether a conversation is archived.
  • How each reply was produced. For each reply we keep a record of the request (time, model, settings, outcome and any error), the text sent to and returned by the model at each step, and the activity of any tools used, such as a web search query or the address of a web page Artemis was asked to read, and what came back.
  • Feedback. If you mark a reply "Helpful" or "Not helpful", we keep that rating, along with any comment.
  • Read aloud. If you use read aloud, we record that you used it and the name of the browser voice. Speech is produced by your own browser; we do not record audio. Voice input and file upload are not enabled.
  • Settings. Your conversation auto-delete setting.
  • Waitlist. If you ask to be told when Artemis's own model is ready, we keep your email address, when you signed up and which page you used.
  • Emails to us. If you email support, we keep the message and your address so we can reply.

Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in cookie. When you sign in we set one cookie, artemis_session (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax), which lasts up to 30 days or until you sign out. On our side we keep only a SHA-256 hash of it, a security (CSRF) token and its expiry time.
  • Security records. To stop password guessing and spam, we record your network (IP) address and the time of each sign-in or sign-up attempt, and count waitlist sign-ups per network address.
  • Service logs. For each call to the Artemis API we record the method, the route with conversation and message IDs removed, the result code, how long it took and a trace ID. These records do not include your account or the content of your messages. Our web server may also keep standard access logs, such as IP address, browser user agent and the page requested. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: whether the gateway's web server keeps access logs, and for how long]
  • Stored in your browser. The site saves a few things in your own browser: your colour palette and motion preference, the model you picked, unsent drafts for your account, a message you typed before signing in (until you sign in), and a cached copy of public pages so they load quickly. These stay on your device and you can clear them in your browser settings.

This website does not load advertising, analytics or social media tracking scripts.

Cutline Industries is training Artemis's own model. Your conversations are not automatically used to train it. If you rate a reply, that reply and the message it answered may be copied, with personal information automatically redacted, into a review set of possible training examples. Nothing in that set is used for training unless consent rules are in place and a person has approved it. 5. Who else handles your information

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We use these service providers to run Artemis:

  • Microsoft Azure (Microsoft Corporation). While Artemis's own model is being trained, Chat's replies are written by a bootstrap model hosted on Microsoft Azure AI services. When you send a message, the conversation context needed for the reply is sent to Microsoft Azure to generate it. The Artemis account service also runs on Microsoft Azure. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: where the account and conversation database is hosted, the Azure region(s) where data is processed and stored, and the Microsoft data processing terms that apply]
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc.). Hosts this website's public pages. GitHub receives the technical details any website host receives, such as your IP address, when you load a page.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC). The site loads its fonts from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts load.
  • Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation). When Artemis uses its web search tool, the search query is sent to Wikipedia from our server.
  • Websites you ask Artemis to read. When Artemis reads a public web page for you, our server requests that page, so the site sees our server rather than you.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or Cutline Industries, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information.

7. Your controls

  • Export: in Chat, open your account and choose "Export my account" to download your account details and every conversation as JSON. You can also export a single conversation as Markdown or JSON.
  • Delete: delete any conversation in Chat, or set auto-delete in your account settings.
  • Close your account or leave the waitlist: email support@artemisai-bycutlineindustries.studio from the address on the account. There is no self-service account deletion yet.
  • Browser storage: clear this site's data in your browser settings at any time.

9. Security and breach notice

We take reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, as the New York SHIELD Act requires. They include storing passwords only as salted scrypt hashes, encrypting traffic with HTTPS, using HttpOnly and Secure sign-in cookies with a separate security token for every change, limiting sign-in attempts, and keeping message content out of our request logs. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the authorities as required by New York General Business Law § 899-aa and other applicable laws. To report a security issue, see security.txt or email support@artemisai-bycutlineindustries.studio.
